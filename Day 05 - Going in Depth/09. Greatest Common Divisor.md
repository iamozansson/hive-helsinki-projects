## Greatest Common Divisor

**Learning Objectives:**

* Reinforce your understanding of loops and how to reduce a problem step by step until a base condition is met.
* Strengthen algorithmic thinking and your understanding of numerical operations.

### Instructions

Write a Go function that takes two integers as input and returns their greatest common divisor (GCD). The GCD is the largest positive integer that divides both of the input integers without leaving a remainder.

}
